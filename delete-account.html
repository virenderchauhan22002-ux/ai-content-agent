AI Content Agent - Delete Account

You can delete your account from the AI Content Agent app.

Steps to delete your account

  1. Open AI Content Agent and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile or Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Read the warning and confirm Delete My Account.

Data deletion

Account deletion is intended to permanently delete your account and associated application data. Any data retained after deletion, and the reason and retention period, must be disclosed accurately by the app developer.

If you cannot delete your account, use the support contact provided in the app.
