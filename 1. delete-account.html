AI Content Agent — Delete Account

You can delete your AI Content Agent account from within the app.

Steps to delete your account

  1. Open AI Content Agent and sign in.
  2. Open Profile or Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Read the warning and confirm Delete My Account.

Data deletion

Account deletion is intended to permanently delete your account and associated application data. Please verify that your account, profile, and any stored content are actually deleted.

If you need help with account deletion, contact the app developer through the support contact provided in the app.
